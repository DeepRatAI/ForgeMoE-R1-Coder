# Public Benchmark Corpus Scan and License Attestation

Step 29.33 performs the first official-source public benchmark attestation gate after hardened data-release integration.

The gate does not train a model and does not ingest raw public benchmark tasks into training. It verifies lightweight official metadata sources, records license/provenance evidence, confirms public benchmarks remain reference/eval-only and upgrades the license basis for Forge-native hardened train candidates from scaffold-only to internal generated synthetic task candidates.

## Source Policy

Public benchmark corpora are reference/eval-only. They are not direct training sources.

Step 29.33 verifies official metadata for:

- HumanEval;
- MBPP;
- BigCodeBench;
- LiveCodeBench;
- SWE-bench;
- SWE-bench Verified;
- SWE-bench Multimodal;
- APPS;
- CodeContests;
- DS-1000;
- RepoBench;
- CRUXEval.

The gate records explicit license metadata when available. Some benchmark datasets expose ambiguous or missing dataset-license metadata even when associated code repositories have licenses. Those benchmarks remain reference/eval-only and never train direct.

## Forge-Native License Attestation

The train candidates from Step 29.32 are internal synthetic tasks generated by ForgeMoE. They do not use raw public benchmark content, external repository snapshots or private heldout content.

This resolves the scaffold-only license blocker for those internal train candidates, but it does not authorize payload materialization yet.

## Remaining Release Blockers

Training-grade data release remains blocked by:

- full public benchmark corpus content snapshotting and fingerprinting;
- training payload materialization authorization.

The next step should perform bounded public benchmark snapshot fingerprinting under budget guardrails and keep all benchmark content out of training payloads.
